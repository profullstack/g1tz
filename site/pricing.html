How a paid plan arrives

  1. An Owner, Admin or Billing Contact links the organization to its OpenAccess principal: g1tz org link ORG oa_…, or on the account page.
  2. Buy the product for that principal at the hub. The product names are in this site's descriptor.
  3. The hub signs an entitlement event to this server; the organization moves to the plan, with the seats the entitlement carries. When the entitlement lapses, the organization returns to Community and keeps its people.

Nothing on this site takes a card. A plan is a fact the hub reports, which is why it travels between apps.
